Help & Guide
How to use the Time Tracker
1Signing in
1
Enter the shared PIN
Ask your supervisor if you don't have it. It only needs re-entering on a device if the PIN is ever changed.
2
Tap your name
Not listed yet? Type it in and tap Add.
3
Confirm "Yes, that's me"
Double-check it's really your name before confirming — this is the easiest place to catch a mistake.
2A normal work day
Your day looks different depending on your job title (set by your manager in Manage Employees) — Operations or Admin.
1
Clock In
Tap it the moment you start — this is what starts your paid time. If your job title is Admin, that's it — you're on the clock with nothing else to fill out, and a Clock Out button is waiting whenever your shift ends.
2
Pre-Trip (Operations only)
Pick your vehicle, confirm the inspection, and confirm you've signed the paper sheet. No vehicle today? Choose "No Vehicle" to skip straight to the next step.
3
Log every job you head to (Operations only)
Search the job by name and tap "I'm heading there." Not on a Mitigation job? Choose Meeting, Cleanings, or Equipment Cleaning from the top of the list, whichever fits. You can switch jobs as many times as you need during the day.
4
Back to the shop (Operations only)
Called back in later? The app offers "Clock Back In" — no need to redo your Pre-Trip.
5
Post-Trip & Clock Out (Operations only)
Confirm the vehicle's clean and fueled, confirm your paper sheet, then Clock Out.
3Long-shift alerts
9 hours — a quick reminder beep
11 hours — a second reminder beep
12 hours — an alarm that keeps sounding until you acknowledge it; contact management for approval before continuing
Admin & Office Guide
Reports, corrections, and settings
1Reports
Everything below lives under the Admin link at the bottom of the screen, inside Reports. What you see there depends on your permissions.
Who's clocked in right now
Daily / Weekly / 2-week Payroll reports
Employee report — by name, any date range
Job report — everyone who worked a job, and a Send button to push hours to Job Tracker
Detailed labor report — per-day cost breakdown including overtime
2Fixing time entries
Requires the Adjust Times permission. Open Admin → Adjust time entries, pick the employee and date.
Edit any shift's clock in/out time
Edit or delete a single job log
Change the entry's date to backdate a missed clock-in or fix the wrong day
Delete the whole day's entry if it was created by mistake
3Managing employees
Edit anyone's name, hourly rate, or permissions anytime
Archive instead of delete — hides them from sign-in but keeps their history intact
Restore an archived employee, or delete permanently if truly needed
4Vehicles, job list & sync
Add or remove vehicles from the Pre-Trip list
Manually add jobs that aren't pulled from Job Tracker
Job Tracker jobs drop off the list automatically 60 days after landing
Hours send to Job Tracker automatically at clock-out, with overtime blended in — the Send button in the Job report is just a backup
5Login PIN & dark mode
Change the shared PIN anytime from Admin → Login PIN — needs the Employees permission. Do this whenever someone leaves the company.
Dark mode is the moon/sun icon in the header — open to everyone, no permission needed.
Login PIN
The shared PIN everyone enters to open the app
Change this any time — for example when someone leaves the company. Everyone will need the new PIN the next time they open the app on a device that isn't already unlocked.
Manage employees
Who's on the sign-in list, and what they can access
Check a box to give that person a shortcut to that section, right on their own screen — no separate login. Leave everything unchecked and they'll never see it. Nothing here is permanent until you tap Save Changes.
